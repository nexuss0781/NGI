# Market analysis

Find out who already does this well, and what they are actually shipping.

## The work

Identify the products in this space that a user would realistically choose
between. For each one, research and write a **separate** section:

- What it is, concretely
- What it does that the others do not
- Where it is genuinely strong
- Where it is genuinely weak — from evidence, not impression
- Who it is for, and who it is not for

## Rules

Evidence over reputation. "Everyone uses it" is not a strength, "it has the
only offline mode in this category" is. If you cannot find a real weakness, say
so rather than inventing one.

Name versions and dates where it matters. A feature that shipped last month is
not the same product as one that shipped two years ago.

## Finishing

One document, one clearly separated section per competitor.
