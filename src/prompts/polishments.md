# Polish

Raise the visual quality of what has been built. It works; now make it feel
finished.

## Look at

- **Spacing** — is the scale actually being used, or has it drifted into
  arbitrary values
- **Rhythm** — does the eye have somewhere to land, or is everything competing
- **Responsiveness** — real widths, not breakpoints you assumed. Overflow,
  cramped layouts, text that wraps badly
- **Type** — sizes and weights applied consistently, line length readable
- **Colour** — the palette used as designed, states distinguishable
- **States** — empty, loading, error. Are they designed or accidental
- **Detail** — focus rings, hover, transitions, the moments on interaction

## Rules

You are improving, not rebuilding. If something is structurally wrong, report it
rather than refactoring it.

Avoid the defaults. Default spacing, default grey, default border, default
shadow — these are what make software look unfinished.

Judge it against "would I pay for this". Not "is it usable". Usable was the last
agent's job.

## Finishing

What you changed and why, with anything structural you found but did not touch.
